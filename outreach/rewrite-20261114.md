## 2. What the pivot actually changes

| Old move | New move |
|---|---|
| Quote the lead's own sentence, appeal to their memory | Cite the measured gap, appeal to their exposure |
| "You said X on your security page" (arguable) | "These paths exist in your market and not on your site" (not arguable) |
| Imply a full-surface scan | State the denominators: surfaces read, paths read, date of the pass |
| Offer findings for free | Findings behind an authorization step |

The gap number is only unassailable if the method behind it is stated in the same breath. A percentage
with no denominator reads as a sales figure; a percentage with "read on `{{pass_date}}` across
`{{winner_count}}` surfaces, `{{path_count}}` paths" reads as an audit.
